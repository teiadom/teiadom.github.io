Research

My work sits where explainable AI meets human-centred computing. I am especially interested in whether explanations from AI systems, including large language models, help people judge when the AI is right, and in building AI that serves users in places like Nigeria, where they are often left out of current research.

Explainable and fair AI Human-centred computing Machine learning Natural language processing Data science Intelligent medical analysis
